Privacy Policy

App: UroDiary  |  Developer: BillionCraft Lab  |  Effective Date: November 26, 2026

1. Overview

BillionCraft Lab ("we", "our", or "us") built UroDiary as a personal hydration, fluid intake, and voiding habit tracking application. We respect your privacy and designed the app to work offline-first with user-controlled data storage.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All logs, timestamps, container presets, notes, and app preferences you enter in UroDiary are stored locally on your Android device. We do not operate external backend servers that collect, view, or store your personal health or habit logs.

3. Optional Google Drive Backup

If you choose to sign in with your Google Account and use the optional Google Drive Backup feature, UroDiary accesses only the dedicated hidden application folder (drive.appdata scope) in your personal Google Drive to create, restore, or delete your backup file. We do not access any other files in your Google Drive, nor do we transfer or sell your backup data to third parties.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your local data remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall UroDiary. If you created a Google Drive backup, you can permanently delete it at any time using the Delete Backup option inside the app or by revoking UroDiary's access in your Google Account security settings. For privacy inquiries, contact us at billioncraftlab.dev@gmail.com.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact BillionCraft Lab at billioncraftlab.dev@gmail.com.
